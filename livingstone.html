A love letter to Livingstone LIVINGSTONE

YOU. You sneaky little minx. I was not expecting you to be ALL that you were. You are crazy, adventurous, fun, raw, natural, genuine...the list goes on and on and on. You are literally everything I could ask for in a lover.

There is a part of myself that I always knew existed, but was really able to explore for the first time when I was with you. The daring, audacious, venturesome part. You really let me be myself, and experience LIFE with no fear. And even when I was afraid, you were there to hold me through the wild ride we were on together.

Not to mention, you are so down-to-earth. You're the type of lover that could rough it out and still have the time of your damn life. Thank you, Livingstone. You showed me what it means to really live. You remind me that life is short, and that there is a reason I am here. Until we meet again. . .

Love, Imaan Selene
