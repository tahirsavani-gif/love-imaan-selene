A love letter to Houston Houston, we have a problem.

Just kidding, haha, I know you're probably so annoyed with the number of times I said that when we were together. In actuality, there was no problem when I was with you.

The truth is, we met for a very specific purpose, and I did not get to experience too much of you outside of that but whatever little I did see was really nice. You were warm, and in many ways, being in your presence felt like a big hug.

I wish I got to see more of you, so I could have more to say, but maybe again another time.

Love,Imaan Selene
