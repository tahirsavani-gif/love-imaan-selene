A love letter to Las Vegas Las Fricken Vegas.

You are insane. You tired me out, in the best way. First of all, can we just talk about your luxe energy? I love how put together you are. Being with you made me feel like a million bucks, and what more could a lady ask for?

Second of all...you really know how to have a good time. I could be mad, sad, anxious, stressed, but if I'm with you, none of it matters. You were the perfect fling; you made me forget about real life and have a good fricken time.

It's so crazy how you brought out sides of me that seem paradoxical; on one hand, it seemed that I had no shame when I was with you, and on another, I still felt like I had my whole life together. You made me feel like a movie star, everywhere I went. Thank you for the confidence you allowed me to experience within myself.

Love,Imaan Selene
