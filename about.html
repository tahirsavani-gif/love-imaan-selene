Dear Readers,

Consider this my pocket page of letters to the world. Letters to the future, letters to the past, letters to myself, letters to you. . . all made with love, by yours truly.

Feel free to explore each corner, from my podcast to my travel series to some of the insight I have gained through my experiences.

I am keen on finding and creating magic in life and approaching the world with a sense of grace and gratitude. It is a continuous journey, and there are times when grace and gratitude feel out of reach. Life can be dramatic, and things can get unfavourable (to say the least). Sometimes, things can feel hopeless. But that is all it is, a feeling, not a truth.

My content is aimed at those of you who want to receive the beauty that life has to offer, even when things do feel hopeless. I hope through the stories I share, you see a little bit of yourself and have the space to reflect, heal, grow and potentially even redefine yourself and your approach to life. After all, we only have one to live...so why not take a shot on yourself?

Magic and beauty are all around us; we just have to stop to notice it. Stick around, and let us uncover it together.

With love, Imaan Selene ♡
